# Keyword Grouper (English + Bahasa Malaysia)

Paste a keyword list – or a Google Keyword Planner export – and get tidy groups for Google Ads ad groups and SEO pages, with search-intent labels and negative keyword ideas.

**Use it online:** https://zenwebmalaysia.github.io/keyword-grouper/

Built by [ZenWeb](https://zenweb.my/), a digital marketing agency in Petaling Jaya, Malaysia (SEO, Google Ads, Meta Ads, web design and development).

## Features

- Groups keywords by their most common shared word, after removing filler words and words you choose to ignore (brand, location, core service).
- Understands common Malay search words: *servis* = service, *harga* / *murah* = price, *pasang* = installation, *cara* = how to.
- Labels intent: **Service** (ready to buy), **Price**, **Local** (near me, PJ, KL…), **Info** (how-to – better for blog posts).
- Flags **negative keyword** ideas: jobs / kerja / jawatan kosong, free / percuma, DIY, courses, PDFs, second-hand.
- Adds up monthly search volume per group when volumes are pasted after a tab or comma.
- Export to CSV or copy for Excel / Google Sheets.

The grouping is simple and transparent rather than AI-based – review the groups and adjust by hand.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is stored or sent anywhere.
