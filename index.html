How to use the groups

For Google Ads

  • Make one ad group per group, named after the group.
  • Write ads that repeat the group’s main words in the headlines.
  • Send each ad group to the most relevant page – the price group to your price list, the repair group to your repair page.
  • Keep Info keywords (“how to…”, “cara…”) out of ads at first. They are cheaper to win with blog posts.
  • Add the negative keywords at campaign level before you launch.

For SEO

  • Each group is usually one page. Don’t write five pages for five versions of the same search – they compete with each other.
  • Use the largest keyword in the page title and main heading, and cover the rest naturally in the content.
  • Turn Info groups into helpful articles that link to your service pages.

Frequently asked questions

How does the grouping work?

The tool finds the words that appear across many keywords (after ignoring filler words and the words you list), then puts each keyword in the group of its most common shared word. It is fast and transparent, but not perfect – read the groups and move the odd keyword by hand.

Does it understand Malay?

Yes for common search words. It treats harga and murah as price searches, servis like service, cara and bagaimana as “how to” searches, and kerja and jawatan kosong as job searches to exclude.

Where do I get keywords?

Google Ads Keyword Planner (free with a Google Ads account), Google Search Console for your own site, the “People also search for” box on Google, and the words customers use on WhatsApp.

Are my keywords sent anywhere?

No. The grouping runs in your browser.
